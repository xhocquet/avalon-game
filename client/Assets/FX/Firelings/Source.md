# Firelings

Imported from the supplied Firelings OBJ export. Attribution and license were not included.

The 6,682 files form 26 repeated groups of 256 embers and one sphere.
There are 92 exact geometry variants after stripping exporter names; the 91
ember variants share the same faces and differ only in scale and float rounding.
One ember and one sphere are retained, centered and scaled to radii 0.07 and 0.5.
Normals and faces are preserved.

No textures, UVs, materials, transforms, or animation settings were supplied.
The scene adds procedural fire, 48 rising ember particles, and a looping pulse.
It loops until removed by its caller.
