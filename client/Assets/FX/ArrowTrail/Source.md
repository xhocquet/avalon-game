# ArrowTrail

Imported from the supplied Test OBJ export. Source attribution and license were not included.

The export contains one angular trail and 72 identical direction arrows, with no
textures, UVs, materials, placements, or animation data. One copy of each mesh is
retained. Vertices are centered and scaled: trail 0.1, arrow 0.25 (one unit long,
pointing along +X). Normals and faces are preserved.

The standalone scenes add a cyan additive material and a one-second fade.
Replay the Effect animation to reuse; the scenes do not free themselves.
