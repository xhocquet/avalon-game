# XP & Leveling

- Progression lives on [`Experience`](../Components/Behaviors/Experience.cs) (`Level`, lifetime `Xp`), added to every hero by `HeroFactory`. It is deliberately not on `Stats` — Stats holds buffable combat values that skills and items write through `Stats.Add`; XP is the input that *produces* those writes.
- [`XpRulesAsset`](../Assets/XpRulesAsset.cs) (row `AssetId: 115`) holds per-victim-type kill rates, the level curve, and the shape of the growth curve. Rates are flat across players; a kill is worth what the victim is worth. **Per-level stat gains are not here** — they are per-hero, on `HeroAsset`, so two heroes can scale differently off the same curve.
- [`StatGrowth`](../StatGrowth.cs) is that curve: `value(level) = base + growth * n * (A + B * n)` with `n = level - 1`. The ramp back-loads the gain, so a late level is worth more than an early one, and `StatGrowthCurveA`/`StatGrowthCurveB` are authored so the ramp hits exactly 1 at the cap — which puts a capped hero on `Base + PerLevel * (MaxLevel - 1)` and is what lets a row be authored straight off a published min-max range. `StatGrowthTests` fails if that pair drifts.
- The curve is an arithmetic series in integers — level 2 costs `XpToSecondLevel`, each level after costs `XpPerLevelIncrement` more. `XpRulesAsset.TotalXpForLevel(level)` is the closed form and is what UI should use for a progress bar.
- **Awarding** happens at the kill site through [`ExperienceRewards.AwardForKill`](../ExperienceRewards.cs), called from `DeathSystem` (everything on the board) and `RespawnSystem.BeginRespawn` (heroes, which never reach `DeathSystem`). Credit goes to **whoever landed the fatal hit** (`Health.LastDamagerUnitId`, recorded by `DamageSystem`) and nowhere else — no team split, no proximity share. Only heroes carry an `Experience`, so a kill credited to a minion or turret pays out nothing. Friendly fire and unattributed deaths award nothing.
- `DeathSystem` resolves the killer once per corpse into `DeadUnitSnapshot` (entity + its `UnitIdentity` + team/owner) and pays every award out in a pass **before** the destroy pass, so a killer that dies on the same tick still earns what it killed. `UnitDiedEvent` carries `DestroyerUnitId`/`DestroyerUnitTypeId` for the view.
- **Spending** happens in [`ExperienceSystem`](../Systems/ExperienceSystem.cs), registered right after `DeathSystem` so a kill lands its level on the same tick. It is the only writer of `Level`, walks every `StatType` and adds `StatGrowth.Between(oldLevel, newLevel)` of the hero's own growth via `Stats.Add`, grows the HP and mana pools through `HealthApplication.GrantMaxHealth` / `ManaApplication.GrantMaxMana` (current HP/mana follow the max up), and raises one `HeroLeveledUpEvent` carrying the level reached even when several levels land at once.
- It adds the **difference between the two levels**, not a per-level step repeated — the curve is not linear, so a multi-level tick would otherwise land somewhere else entirely. The base cancels out of that difference, which is why no base/bonus split is needed and item bonuses simply stack on top.
- XP is lifetime-earned and never reset, so it survives death and respawn for free — the hero entity is never destroyed.
